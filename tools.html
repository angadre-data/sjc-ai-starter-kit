Which AI should I use?

Start with the task, not the tool.

There is no single best AI tool. The best choice depends on what you are trying to do, what context you have, and where the output needs to go next.

✅
New to AI tools? Start with Gemini.

Gemini is available to all SJC staff through Google Workspace — no approval needed. It's governed by our existing Google security and privacy controls, making it the safest starting point for everyday tasks like drafting, summarizing, and research.

ChatGPT and Claude are also approved at SJC but require permission from your leader before use.
