Help build the library

The best examples come from real work.

If AI helped you save time, improve a draft, spot a mistake, or rethink a workflow — add it here so others can learn from it. Small examples are welcome. Useful failures are especially welcome.

📤
How to submit

Use the Google Form below to submit a recipe, aha moment, or tool note. The templates on this page show you what information to prepare before filling in the form.

Questions about AI policy or tools at SJC? Post in #ai-committee on Slack or email sjc.connect@stjoseph.com.

Submit via Google Form ↗

Use the templates below to prepare your submission before opening the form.

Submit a recipe

Use this template to share a workflow that worked. It does not need to be perfect — the goal is to make it repeatable for others.

Recipe name: Team: Tool(s) used: Status: [Ready to use / Pilot / Exploring] Difficulty: [Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced] Estimated time saved: Use when: [Describe the situation where this recipe is useful] Inputs needed: - - Steps: 1. 2. 3. Prompt (copy-paste ready): [Paste your prompt here, using [BRACKETS] for the fields someone would replace] Example output summary: [Describe or paste a sample of what good output looks like] Human check steps: - - What changed or what was the impact: [Optional: time saved, quality improvement, or unexpected result]

Submit an aha moment

An aha can be a better prompt, a useful failure, a surprising result, or a 15-minute win. The format is the same regardless of how big or small it was.

Team: Title (one sentence — what did you learn?): What I tried: [Describe what you were trying to do and how you used AI] What surprised me: [What was unexpected — good or bad] What worked: [What approach or prompt produced useful output] What failed: [What did not work, and why you think it failed] What I would do differently next time: [Your recommendation for others] Prompt or output link (if shareable): [Optional]

Submit a tool note

Found a tool that saved you time, or discovered a watchout others should know about? Add it here.

Tool name: Category: [LLM / Research / Transcription / Translation / Video / Design / Analytics / Automation / Other] Team: Status: [Using / Exploring] Pricing: [Free / Free tier + paid / Paid only] What it's good for: - - Common uses at SJC: - Watchouts: - Recommendation: [Would you recommend this tool to other teams? Why or why not?]

Submissions are reviewed weekly. Approved entries are added to the library within a few days.
